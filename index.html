FAQs

Quick answers to common questions.

You typically need: Identity proof (Aadhar/PAN), Address proof, Income proof (salary slips or ITR), Bank statements (last 6 months), Property documents, and passport-size photographs.
Most banks require a minimum CIBIL score of 650–700. Private banks like HDFC and ICICI prefer 750+. A higher score means better interest rates and faster approval.
Processing typically takes 3–7 working days for salaried applicants. Home loans may take 2–4 weeks due to property verification. Personal and gold loans can be approved within 24–48 hours.
Yes! Eligibility depends on your income-to-EMI ratio (FOIR). With a salary of ₹20,000+/month, you can qualify for several loan types. Co-applicants can strengthen your application significantly.
Secured loans (Home, Vehicle, Gold) require collateral and offer lower interest rates. Unsecured loans (Personal, Consumer Durable) don't need collateral but come with higher rates and stricter credit checks.
Maintain a CIBIL score above 750, reduce existing EMI obligations, add a co-applicant with stable income, show consistent bank transaction history, and avoid multiple loan applications simultaneously.
FOIR (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio) is the percentage of your monthly income going towards EMIs. Most banks allow a maximum FOIR of 40–55%. A lower FOIR means more eligibility for additional loans.
