❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is LoanMani?
LoanMani is your AI-powered loan assistant that helps you check eligibility, understand loan types, and predict default risk using advanced ML models.
How accurate is the risk evaluator?
Our Random Forest model achieves 0.812 ROC-AUC on 800 historical applications, calibrated to 2026 Indian bank underwriting criteria.
Is my data safe?
All data stays in your browser session. Nothing is stored on our servers. Your privacy is our priority.
Which banks are supported?
SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Bank of Baroda, PNB, Cosmos, and Saraswat Co-operative Bank — plus the general RBI lending benchmark.
